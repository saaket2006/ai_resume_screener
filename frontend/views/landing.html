Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Resume Match Score differ from keyword match? ▼
Keyword matching requires exact text matching. Our Semantic Matching engine parses aliases (e.g. JavaScript ↔ JS) and understands structural connections in the JD context, ensuring highly qualified candidates aren't filtered out due to terminology differences.
Can I customize the component weights? ▼
Yes! Recruiters can resolve different Scoring Profiles (e.g., DevOps Engineer, AI Engineer, Fresh Graduate) which adjusts the component coefficients for Skills, Experience, Education, and Projects dynamically.
